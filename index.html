ResearchMSc thesis · part 2/3

Problem
Route averages treat every shipment alike. They miss how carriers, ports and shipments influence each other over time.
Built
A heterogeneous temporal graph network in PyTorch Geometric: 5 node types with type-specific GRU memory, factored route embeddings, 3 task encoders and 8 prediction heads, trained with a 4-stage curriculum.
Result
Beat the route-median baseline across 787,957 test predictions (14.53 vs 14.83 days MAE), with error cut by 60% on road and 34% on rail.
PyTorch 2.6PyTorch GeometricCUDA / AMP FP16AdamW

ResearchMSc thesis · 2026

Problem
Freight transit times are hard to predict. The data is dirty, and a few shipments take far longer than most.
Built
A 14-step cleaning pipeline that cut 8.2M shipment-leg records to 3.4M usable ones. A graph with five node types, and a temporal graph network with 8 output heads and 22 engineered features. A dashboard that explains predictions and lets you try "what if" scenarios.
Result
Mean absolute error of 14.53 days, against 14.83 for a route-median baseline. Main finding: data quality mattered more than the model's architecture.
PyTorch GeometricPolarsDuckDBStreamlit
ResearchMSc thesis · part 1/3

Problem
About half of the raw shipment legs carried placeholder or fabricated timestamps. A model trained on them would learn noise.
Built
A 14-step cleaning pipeline in DuckDB and Polars over 6 relational tables: filters for unknown legs and predicted timestamps, a year-error repair, container de-duplication and event-group leg pairing.
Result
8.2M raw legs became 3.4M clean legs and 10.2M time-ordered graph interactions. Usable training labels rose from 18.1% to 39.6%.

RoboticsStudy · 2025

Problem
The stepper motors on an igus gantry robot ran jerky and loud, with a resonance zone at 300–600 RPM.
Built
Two test series on the real rig in TwinCAT 3 over EtherCAT: a drive-frequency sweep from 1 to 32 kHz, then step-by-step tuning of Kv, Kp, lag monitoring and cycle time, each run logged with TwinCAT Scope and plotted in MATLAB.
Result
Very jerky at 1 kHz, smooth and quiet from 8 kHz without load; a tuned parameter set also ran smoothly at 2 kHz.
TwinCAT 3Beckhoff EtherCATTwinCAT ScopeMATLAB
RoboticsStudy · 2025

Problem
In highway video the camera moves too, and a detector boxes every car, parked or moving.
Built
A MATLAB vision pipeline: YOLOv4 finds objects, SURF feature matching and an affine warp cancel the camera's own motion, and frame differencing with morphology marks what really moves.
Result
Only moving cars, trucks and people get a labelled box; parked cars and buildings are filtered out.
